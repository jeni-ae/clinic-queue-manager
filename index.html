    Inside the queue: a circular array with 10 slots

    How the queue works

    A queue is first in, first out (FIFO): the person who joins first is served first. New people join at the rear and are called from the front.

    • enqueue adds a patient at the rear. If all 10 slots are used, the queue is full.
    • dequeue removes the patient at the front. If no one is waiting, the queue is empty.
    • peek shows who is next without removing them.
    • The array is circular: after slot 9 the rear wraps back to slot 0, so freed slots are reused.
